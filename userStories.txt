Project name: E-commerce app
Group: group 4
Group member: Santhra Thomas, Nguyen Le, Duyet Nguyen
User stories:
1.	Customer can log in to the app with username and password.
2.	A new customer can register an account to log in.
3.  Customer can reset their password if forgotten through the password reset page.
4.	Customer can update their profile.
5.	Customer can browse a list of products.
6.	Customer can add and remove products to/from the shopping cart.
7.	Customer can check out the products.
8. The customer can confirm whether the transaction was successful or not through a pop-up message.
9.	As an admin, I can add a new product to the stock.
10.	Admin can update a product price.
11.	Admin can update product quantity.
12.	Admin can print out the customer's shopping history.
13.	Admin can review and print out the customer's info.
