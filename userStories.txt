Project name: E-commerce app
Group: group 4
Group member: Santhra Thomas, Nguyen Le, Duyet Nguyen
User stories:
1.	Customer can log in the app with username and password.
2.	New customer can register an account to login.
3.	Customer can update profile.
4.	Customer wants to browse a list of products.
5.	Customer adds a product to the shopping cart.
6.	Customer will checkout.
7.	As an admin, I can add a new product to the stock.
8.	Admin can update a product price.
9.	Admin wants to update product price.
10.	Admin has the ability to update product quantity.
11.	Admin wants to print out customer shopping history.
12.	Admin can review and print out customer info.
